Blitz Split

Split the bill. Keep the friends.

Live

An offline-first app to split bills among friends. Kotlin Multiplatform is shared up to the ViewModel, with a native UI on each platform: Jetpack Compose on Android, SwiftUI with Liquid Glass on Apple platforms and React on the web.

  • Every bill is saved on the device first and synced through Supabase, with an optional account.
  • Splits equally, by amounts, by percentages or item by item.
  • Fills a bill in from a photo of the receipt.
  • Available in 43 languages (45 locales).
Platforms
Android, iOS, macOS and web
Stack
Kotlin Multiplatform, Jetpack Compose, SwiftUI, React, Supabase
Google Play blitzsplit.com Source code

Bible Planner

Your whole Bible, one day at a time.

Live, with paying users

A Kotlin Multiplatform app for Android, iOS and desktop, built with Compose Multiplatform from a single codebase shared across all three.

  • Reading plans in chronological or book order, with daily progress tracking.
  • Highlights and notes synced to your account.
  • A daily study, powered by AI: a summary of the passage, its historical context and reflection questions.
  • A chat assistant that already knows the day's reading.
Platforms
Android, iOS and desktop
Stack
Kotlin Multiplatform, Compose Multiplatform, Supabase, RevenueCat, Firebase
